Difference decoder: skip the empty shared-prefix pass
=====================================================

Change: ObStringDiffDecoder::batch_decode() ran a full pass over the row batch
copying the shared (non-diff) bytes into each row buffer. When every descriptor
in the header marks a differing range that pass copies nothing, which is the
normal case for the short document-id values used by the FTS retrieval path.
The pass is now skipped unless the header actually carries shared bytes.

Paired microbench, 1000 queries per sample, 12 alternating pairs per
orientation. Delta is candidate versus the previous commit; negative means the
candidate is faster.

  forward (2881 previous commit, 2882 candidate)
    cn      median -2.44%   mean -3.11%   8/12 candidate faster
    beng    median -4.49%   mean -4.45%   9/12
    mixed   median -0.95%   mean -1.42%   7/12
    limit   median -1.44%   mean -3.44%   6/12

  swapped (2881 candidate, 2882 previous commit)
    cn      median +0.74%   mean +0.03%
    beng    median -0.83%   mean +0.12%
    mixed   median -3.64%   mean -4.90%
    limit   median +0.19%   mean +0.86%

The swapped run is expressed in the same frame, so a negative value there means
the previous commit was faster. The two orientations disagree by a few percent,
which is the local noise floor. Removing the port bias from the pair of runs
leaves roughly -1.6% (cn), -1.9% (beng), +1.4% (mixed), -0.8% (limit); the
per-port bias itself is about 1-2%. The effect is small and the decision belongs
to the reference harness, whose query stdev is around 0.5% rather than 2-4%.
